Disclosure

How AI is used here

AI is used throughout this site: in research, in writing, and in building the software.

The short version

The methodology is mine. I use AI anyway, because one person cannot hold every detail at once. A second set of eyes on every claim is better than one set of eyes and confidence.

What AI does

  • Drafts and edits the lesson copy.
  • Checks claims against the source material.
  • Writes and tests the site's code.
  • Cross-references statistics against their sources.

What AI does not do

It does not invent the methodology. It does not generate the statistics. It does not decide what counts as an edge. Those come from my own trade record and the Hypothesis Register.

What I verify by hand

  • Every setup definition.
  • Every statistic, and its sample size.
  • Every rule.

Built to be read by your tools

This site is built to be AI-readable, so you can point your own tools at it and work with the material your way.

llms.txt sits at the site root: a plain-text guide to every page, written for the tools that read them.

The Setup is the edge. Discipline is the execution. Everything else is noise.
