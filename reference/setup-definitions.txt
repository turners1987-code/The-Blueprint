=== 9 EMA Pullback Entry ===
Context: Confirmed trend already established (via stacked imbalance/footprint absorption,
HTF structure, or price action). Not a standalone read - this is an entry trigger layered
onto an existing Trend Continuation or missed-stack scenario.
Trigger: Price pulls back to the 9 EMA (1-min chart). Confirmation is either (a) a wick
through the EMA followed by a close back on the trend side, or (b) a full close through the
EMA against trend, followed by a close back beyond it in trend direction on a subsequent
bar. Either counts as rejection/reclaim.
Stop: Above (short) or below (long) the nearest relevant structure - swing high/low or key
level, discretionary judgment call. Keep it simple for now; revisit only if it starts
costing you.
Target: Next structural level in trend direction - today that was ON Low. Standard 2R target
otherwise.
Management: Trail to swing structure as trend extends (same trail logic you already used
today - 2 consecutive closes beyond active trendline/EMA still applies as your exit signal
on a runner).

=== Failed Breakdown/Breakout ===
Failed Breakdown is Bearish. Failed Breakout is Bullish. Price puts in some sort of
manipulation below a level, exact criteria is 2 closes on the 1 min chart... then buys back
above. Enter on a pause n go

=== Gap Fill ===
trade from the open til the gap fills

=== No Set Up ===
Trading based off feel. Gambling.

=== Pause n Go ===
price shows absorption in the footprint - or - blue orders holding it up (long) or pink
holding it down (short) - before continuing in the direction toward the next target.

=== Range Rejection ===
Fade a push into a named range extreme (ON High, PD High, VAH) when price opens inside prior
day range and value area. Confirmed by shooting star candle(s) at the level, dominant pink
footprint, minimal blue orders on pullback, and bullish candle with wick piercing 9 EMA
closing below it as entry signal. Entry at break of that candle's low. Stop above candle
high. Target 2R.

=== Stacked Imbalance Setup ===
Context: 5-min footprint (Bid x Ask) shows a stacked imbalance cluster - multiple adjacent
price levels with sustained one-sided imbalance (per current settings: ratio 5, min delta 15)
plus sustained negative or positive bar delta / cumulative delta trending in one direction
across the sequence. This is the location - the level(s) where the setup can trigger.
Regime read (hypothesis, not yet validated): Trending/momo day -> enter early, at or near the
stack itself (Trend Continuation logic). Price is unlikely to give a clean retest.
Chop/range day -> wait for a pullback/retest of the stack before entering (Range Rejection
logic, Dale's method). Missed the stack entirely -> fall back to the 9 EMA Pullback Entry on
the subsequent trend continuation.
Confirmation: Price revisits the stacked-imbalance zone and gets rejected rather than
reclaiming it - Trapped Traders signal on the footprint. Rejection on revisit is the trigger
to act (or, on a trending day, the stack forming with sustained delta is itself the trigger
without waiting for a revisit).
Entry trigger: Pause N Go or 9 EMA Pullback Entry, whichever fits the depth of the move at
that moment - Stacked Imbalance is the read/location, not the trigger itself.
Stop: Above (short) or below (long) nearest structure - swing high/low or key level,
discretionary.
Target: Next structural level in trend direction (key level, ON High/Low, etc.).
Management: Trail to swing structure as trend extends; 2 consecutive closes beyond active
trendline/9 EMA = flatten runner.
Open item: Ratio 5 setting is untested against MNQ-specific data - origin was fewer signals
on screen, not backtested. If a loss shows an un-colored stack nearby, that's a flag the
setting may be too tight.
