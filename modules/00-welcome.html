What Is The Blueprint?

The Blueprint is a personal trading framework built around one instrument — MNQ (Micro E-mini Nasdaq-100 Futures) — and a small set of setups the author is building and testing in the open.

This course writes that framework down. Vocabulary is introduced as it is needed, and the glossary is there when it is not. Each claim carries its evidence status, and most of it is still candidate-grade. Nothing here is presented as proven that isn't.

This is not a get-rich-quick course. Trading is a skill. Like any skill, it takes time, repetition, and honest self-review to develop. What this course gives you is a framework — a clear, rules-based system for making decisions. What you do with it is up to you.

Who This Is For

  • Anyone who wants to learn the framework, whatever their prior experience
  • Anyone curious about how day trading actually works
  • People interested in futures specifically. The framework is taught on MNQ; the concepts are not instrument-specific, and nothing here claims they work on other instruments
  • Anyone who wants a real framework, not YouTube hype

How This Course Works

The Blueprint is structured as a progressive learning path. Each module builds on the last. We introduce concepts in the order you actually need them — not alphabetically, not randomly, but in the sequence that makes them click.

Foundation First

Modules 01–03 cover how claims are labelled, the three tiers of idea, setup and trigger, and what counts as a level. The setups come after that context.

Setups Layer In

Modules 04–09 cover Trend Continuation, Range Rejection, the Gap Setups, Triggers, Trade Construction and the Hard Rules. Modules 10–13 are the daily process; 14–16 are the capstone.

What You'll Need

Right now? Nothing. Just read and learn. Later, when you're ready to practice:

  • A brokerage account, once you want to practise
  • A charting platform — we use NinjaTrader for MNQ
  • A trading journal — Module 12 covers the habit
  • A pen and paper doesn't hurt — write things down
One Rule Before You Begin

Never risk money you cannot afford to lose. Trading involves real risk. Risk is covered in Modules 08 and 09, but this reminder lives here, at the very beginning, on purpose.

The Philosophy in One Line

"The Setup is the edge.
Discipline is the execution.
Everything else is noise."

Everything in this course comes back to that. Whether a given setup has an edge is a separate question, and the course states where each one stands.
