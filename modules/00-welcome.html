Why You Are Here

You may know the pattern. A setup from a video looked clean on the replay, so you traded it. Some days it paid, some days it didn't, and a month later you couldn't say whether it was the setup, the market or you, because nothing was written down: not the rule, not the stop, not the reason for the exit.

That is the problem this course answers. Without a written rule and a record, every result is a story, and you can't improve a story. The course gives you the rules, the vocabulary and the routines to turn your trading into a record you can read.

What Is The Blueprint?

The Blueprint is a trading framework written down so you can build your own: levels, reads, triggers, construction, rules and routines, illustrated on MNQ.

This course writes that framework down. Vocabulary is introduced as it is needed, and the glossary is there when it is not. The framework is written down here so you can learn it, test it against your own record, and keep what holds.

This is not a get-rich-quick course. Trading is a skill. Like any skill, it takes time, repetition, and honest self-review to develop. What this course gives you is a framework — a clear, rules-based system for making decisions. What you do with it is up to you.

Who This Is For

  • Anyone who wants to learn the framework and test it on their own record
  • Anyone curious about how day trading actually works
  • Intraday traders in futures. The framework is illustrated on MNQ and written to carry across futures instruments; your record decides where it holds
  • Anyone who wants a written framework they can test

How This Course Works

The Blueprint is structured as a progressive learning path. Each module builds on the last. Concepts arrive in the order you actually need them — not alphabetically, not randomly, but in the sequence that makes them click.

Foundation First

Module 01 covers how to check a claim. Modules 02 and 03 come next: the hard rules that keep an account alive, and the mechanics of the instrument.

Modules 04–07 cover why price moves: who is forced to trade, the auction, what counts as a level, and order flow.

Setups Layer In

Modules 08–15 are the Blueprint itself: the three tiers, idea generation, the setups, triggers, trade construction and the written plan.

Modules 16–20 are the daily process; 21–24 are the evidence and the capstone.

What You'll Need

Right now? Nothing. Just read and learn. Later, when you're ready to practise:

  • A brokerage account, once you want to practise
  • A charting platform — the course uses NinjaTrader 8, with MNQ for its examples
  • A trading journal — Module 18 covers the habit
  • A pen and paper doesn't hurt — write things down
One Rule Before You Begin

Never risk money you cannot afford to lose. Trading involves real risk. Risk is covered in Modules 02 and 14, but this reminder lives here, at the very beginning, on purpose.

The Philosophy

The Setup is the plan.
Discipline is the execution.
Everything else is noise.

— The Blueprint

Everything in this course comes back to that. Whether a setup holds up for you is a question your own record answers; the course teaches how to ask it, and shows no trading results of its own.

Where Do You Stand?

Everyone reads Modules 01 to 03 in order. This check tells you which of them to read slowly. Answer yes or no to each line, about your trading as it is today:

  1. You have a daily loss limit, written down and set at your broker, that stops you trading for the day.
  2. You know the hours you are allowed to trade and the news releases you stay flat for.
  3. You have never moved a stop further away after the trade was on.
  4. You can say, without looking, what one tick and one point of your contract are worth.
  5. You know which contract month you trade and the date you have to roll out of it.
  6. You can turn a stop distance in points into a dollar risk per contract in your head.

The threshold: any "no" on lines 1 to 3 means Module 02, The Hard Rules, comes before your next trade, live or sim. All three "yes" but any "no" on lines 4 to 6 means your gap is the instrument, and Module 03, The Instrument, is the one to work through line by line. Six of six: read both anyway; the rest of the course assumes every line.

Before Your Next Session

Apply This Now

Write one line on paper: the most you will lose in one trading day. Keep it where you trade. That's the whole assignment; Module 02 turns that line into a rule.
