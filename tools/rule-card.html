Rule Card

The checks you answer before the order, the one table that sets the size, and the limit that ends the day. Every line is taken from the modules.

All tools

1. Before the order: the four grade checks

Answer each yes or no at the moment of the order (Module 14).

  1. The location is a named level.
  2. The trigger fired before entry.
  3. The stop is behind structure.
  4. The size matches the plan for that stop.

A+ is all four with a fill within 2 ticks of the intended price; A is all four; B is three of four with no breach of the stop or size check; C is two or fewer, or any breach of those two. C is no trade.

3. Is your trade constructed?

  1. Target: a level with its price, written before entry.
  2. Stop: behind the far edge by your written distance, or at your floor if that was wider.
  3. Ratio: room ÷ risk worked out at the intended price, and 2 or more.
  4. Size: the table's share of base R ÷ (stop ticks × tick value), rounded down, with the arithmetic written.
  5. Entry: the intended price written, and the stop, scale and target sent with the order.
  6. Hands off: after entry, no stop or target moved except the planned scale and a thesis invalidation written at entry.

If the ratio line is under 2, the fifth line reads "no trade".

4. The hard rules

  • Window: no execution outside 06:00 to 16:00 ET, and no new entries from 15:55 ET (Module 02).
  • News: Tier One, flat at T−5, no entries until T+2. Tier Two, manage through.
  • Flatten by hand: four reasons to flatten by hand. Every other manual exit is logged Too Early.
  • Stop: widening the stop is Broke Rule, every time.
  • Reset: after every loss, the full reset before any new entry.

5. The kill switch

Daily max loss is 2R, fixed, set at the account level with the lock on wherever the venue allows, never raised intraday. It is set at the broker or prop-firm level, so it fires whether or not you agree with it. There is no override and there are no size-down ladders. At 2R: close the platform. The setup steps are in Setting Up NinjaTrader 8.

The Setup is the plan. Discipline is the execution. Everything else is noise.
