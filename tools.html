Tools

NinjaTrader 8 tools

Indicators and add-ons that put the setups to work on your charts. Downloads open as each tool is finished.

Requirements

These tools require NinjaTrader 8 on Windows. Some also require the Order Flow+ add-on, marked on each card.

Templates are supplied separately

Chart templates and Trading Hours templates do not travel inside a NinjaScript export. They are supplied separately with each tool.

How to install
  1. Open the NinjaTrader 8 Control Center.
  2. Choose Tools, then Import, then NinjaScript Add-On.
  3. Select the downloaded zip file.
